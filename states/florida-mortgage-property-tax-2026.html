How Florida Property Taxes Impact Your 2026 Mortgage

Florida offers an attractive ~0.82% average effective property tax rate in 2026 paired with 0% state income tax and the famous 'Save Our Homes' constitutional cap, which limits annual assessed value increases on homesteaded primary residences to 3% (or CPI, whichever is lower) plus up to a $50,000 Homestead Exemption. However, Florida's true monthly housing wildcard is hurricane homeowners insurance—which averages $3,800–$5,000+/year on a $500,000 home in South and Coastal Florida.

Real $500,000 Monthly Payment Breakdown in Florida

On a $500,000 home in Florida at 6.5% APR, property taxes average a modest ~$342/month ($4,100/year), yet homeowners insurance can easily match or exceed property tax at ~$350/month ($4,200/year). With 20% down, all-in monthly PITI runs roughly $3,220/month—or ~$3,780/month with 10% down including PMI. Additionally, Florida condo buyers must carefully inspect post-Surfside Milestone Structural Reserve HOA assessments, which can add $400–$900+/month on older coastal buildings.

4 Smart Ways Florida Homebuyers Cut Monthly Costs in 2026

  • File your Florida Homestead Exemption before March 1 following your purchase year to lock in the $50,000 exemption and the 3% Save Our Homes annual assessment cap.
  • Order a Wind Mitigation Inspection ($100–$150) immediately—impact windows, hip roofs, and roof-to-wall clips can slash Florida homeowners insurance premiums by 25%–45%!
  • If moving to a new primary home within Florida, use Save Our Homes 'Portability' to transfer up to $500,000 of accumulated tax-assessment savings to your next house.
  • Always budget the previous owner's property tax reset: when you buy a home in Florida, the county resets assessed value to your new purchase price the following January.

Frequently Asked Questions: Florida Mortgage & Property Taxes

Why do Florida property taxes jump the year after I buy a house?

Under Save Our Homes, the previous seller's assessed value was capped at 3%/yr for years. When ownership transfers, the county resets the home's assessed value to current market price—so always estimate tax on YOUR purchase price, not the seller's old bill.

How much is a $500,000 mortgage per month in Florida in 2026?

With 20% down at 6.5% APR, expect roughly $3,220/month ($2,528 P&I + $342 property tax + ~$350 insurance), excluding HOA fees.

Does Florida charge state income tax on my paycheck or home sale?

No. Florida has 0% state income tax and 0% state capital gains tax.

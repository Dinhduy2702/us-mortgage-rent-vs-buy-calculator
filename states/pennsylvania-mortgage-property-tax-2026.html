How Pennsylvania Property Taxes Impact Your 2026 Mortgage

Pennsylvania averages a ~1.38% effective property tax rate in 2026, though rates vary dramatically by school district—from ~0.95% inside Philadelphia (where the city relies more on its 3.75% wage tax) up to 1.8%–2.3% in top suburban Main Line, Bucks, and Allegheny County school districts. Pennsylvania also enforces one of the highest real estate transfer taxes in the US (2% statewide/local combined, and over 4% in Philadelphia and Pittsburgh, typically split 50/50 between buyer and seller).

Real $500,000 Monthly Payment Breakdown in Pennsylvania

On a $500,000 home in Pennsylvania at 6.5% APR with 20% down, property taxes average ~$575/month ($6,900/year) and insurance runs ~$146/month—bringing total monthly PITI to roughly $3,249/month, or ~$3,809/month with 10% down including PMI. For Philadelphia new construction buyers, Philly's 10-year tax abatement (phased down 10% per year on improvements) can still cut Year-1 property taxes dramatically.

How Virginia Property Taxes Impact Your 2026 Mortgage

Virginia offers one of the most balanced, predictable homeownership tax profiles on the East Coast in 2026, with a statewide effective property tax rate averaging just 0.76% (roughly 1.0%–1.1% in Northern Virginia's Fairfax, Arlington, and Loudoun counties, and 0.55%–0.85% across Richmond, Charlottesville, and Hampton Roads) plus affordable homeowners insurance (~$1,650/year on a $500k home).

Real $500,000 Monthly Payment Breakdown in Virginia

On a $500,000 home in Virginia at 6.5% APR with 20% down, monthly property tax averages just ~$317/month ($3,800/year) and insurance runs ~$138/month—bringing total all-in PITI to roughly $2,983/month, or ~$3,543/month with 10% down including PMI. For D.C. metro commuters, Virginia's combination of 0.76%–1.05% property tax, zero county income tax (unlike Maryland), and 5.75% state income tax cap keeps monthly housing and paycheck taxes significantly leaner than Maryland or D.C.

Frequently Asked Questions: Virginia Mortgage & Property Taxes

How much are property taxes on a $500,000 home in Virginia in 2026?

At the 0.76% statewide average, property taxes run roughly $3,800/year (~$317/month), or ~$5,000–$5,500/year (~$420–$460/mo) in Fairfax and Arlington counties.

How much is a $500,000 mortgage in Virginia with 20% down at 6.5%?

Total monthly PITI averages ~$2,983/month ($2,528 P&I + $317 property tax + $138 insurance).

Do VA loans in Virginia require monthly PMI?

No! Eligible service members and veterans using a VA loan pay $0 monthly PMI even with 0% down (only a one-time VA funding fee, which is waived for eligible disabled veterans).

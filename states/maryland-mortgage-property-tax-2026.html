How Maryland Property Taxes Impact Your 2026 Mortgage

Maryland averages a clean ~1.00% effective property tax rate in 2026 across Montgomery, Howard, Anne Arundel, and Prince George's counties (while Baltimore City carries a notably higher ~2.2% municipal rate). Maryland protects primary homeowners with its Homestead Tax Credit—capping annual taxable assessment increases at 10% statewide and as low as 0%–5% in many counties (e.g., 4% in Baltimore City, 5% in Howard/Prince George's, 10% in Montgomery).

Real $500,000 Monthly Payment Breakdown in Maryland

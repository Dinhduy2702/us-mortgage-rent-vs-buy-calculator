How Arizona Property Taxes Impact Your 2026 Mortgage

Arizona delivers one of the lowest effective property tax rates in America in 2026 at just 0.56%—backed by Proposition 117, a constitutional cap limiting annual Limited Property Value (LPV) increases to 5% per year! Paired with Arizona's rock-bottom 2.50% flat state income tax, Phoenix, Scottsdale, Gilbert, and Tucson homeowners enjoy dramatically lower monthly carrying costs than almost any other major US metro.

Real $500,000 Monthly Payment Breakdown in Arizona

On a $500,000 home in Phoenix or Scottsdale at 6.5% APR with 20% down, property taxes average a tiny ~$233/month ($2,800/year) and insurance runs ~$158/month—bringing total all-in PITI to just ~$2,920/month! That is nearly $700/month LESS than New Jersey and $550/month LESS than Texas on the exact same $500,000 home price. With 10% down including PMI, initial monthly cost is ~$3,480/month before dropping to ~$3,236/month once LTV hits 80%.

4 Smart Ways Arizona Homebuyers Cut Monthly Costs in 2026

  • Confirm your county assessor classifies your home as Class 3 (Primary Residence) rather than Class 4 (Rental/Second Home) to receive Arizona's homeowner school-tax reduction.
  • Watch HOA dues in master-planned Phoenix/Scottsdale communities—while property tax is tiny ($233/mo), resort-style HOA + community fees can add $150–$400/month.

Frequently Asked Questions: Arizona Mortgage & Property Taxes

How much are property taxes on a $500,000 home in Arizona (Phoenix) in 2026?

At Arizona's 0.56% effective average, property taxes on a $500,000 home run just ~$2,800/year (~$233/month).

What is the monthly mortgage on a $500,000 Arizona home with 20% down?

At 6.5% APR, total monthly PITI averages ~$2,920/month ($2,528 P&I + $233 property tax + $158 insurance)—one of the lowest in America.

Does Arizona cap annual property tax value increases?

Yes. Proposition 117 caps annual increases in your home's Limited Property Value (LPV) at 5% per year regardless of how fast market prices surge.

How Oregon Property Taxes Impact Your 2026 Mortgage

Oregon averages a moderate ~0.86% effective property tax rate in 2026—governed by Oregon's Measure 5 and Measure 50, which cap annual Maximum Assessed Value (MAV) growth at 3.0% per year AND do NOT reset (uncap) your tax base when a home is sold! That means an older Portland or Eugene home can carry a surprisingly low tax bill compared to a brand-new home of the exact same market price.

Real $500,000 Monthly Payment Breakdown in Oregon

On a $500,000 home in Portland, Beaverton, or Bend at 6.5% APR with 20% down, property taxes average ~$358/month ($4,300/year) and insurance runs ~$129/month—putting total monthly PITI near ~$3,016/month, or ~$3,576/month with 10% down including PMI. Always inspect the exact county tax record on any Oregon listing: because Measure 50 never resets on sale, two $500k homes in Portland can easily differ by $200+/month in property taxes.

4 Smart Ways Oregon Homebuyers Cut Monthly Costs in 2026

  • Check the property's actual Maximum Assessed Value (MAV) vs. Real Market Value (RMV) before buying—under Measure 50, you inherit the seller's capped 3% MAV tax base as long as you don't do major permitted additions!
  • Remember major renovations (adding square footage or a full remodel) trigger an 'exception value' reassessment in Oregon, raising your MAV permanently.

Frequently Asked Questions: Oregon Mortgage & Property Taxes

Do Oregon property taxes reset to my purchase price when I buy a house?

No! Unlike California (Prop 13) or Florida (Save Our Homes), Oregon's Measure 50 does NOT reset Maximum Assessed Value upon sale—you inherit the home's existing 3%-capped tax base.

How much are property taxes on a $500,000 home in Oregon (Portland) in 2026?

At the 0.86% statewide average, property taxes run roughly $4,300/year (~$358/month), though Multnomah County (Portland) averages closer to 1.0%–1.1%.

What is the monthly payment on a $500,000 Oregon home with 20% down?

At 6.5% APR, total monthly PITI averages ~$3,016/month ($2,528 P&I + $358 property tax + $129 insurance).

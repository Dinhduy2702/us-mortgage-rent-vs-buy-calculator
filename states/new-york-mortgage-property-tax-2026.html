How New York Property Taxes Impact Your 2026 Mortgage

New York's effective property tax rate averages roughly 1.40% statewide in 2026, but the spread between New York City and suburban counties is huge. Inside the five boroughs of NYC, effective property tax rates on single-family homes and condos often sit near 0.90%–1.10% (though monthly condo common charges/HOA and co-op maintenance fees are steep). Meanwhile, Long Island (Nassau/Suffolk) and Hudson Valley/Westchester suburbs frequently see effective property tax rates of 1.9%–2.5%.

Real $500,000 Monthly Payment Breakdown in New York

On a $500,000 home in New York at the 1.40% statewide average, property tax adds ~$583/month ($7,000/year) and homeowners insurance adds ~$163/month—bringing a 20%-down 6.5% mortgage to roughly $3,274/month all-in, or ~$3,834/month with 10% down including PMI. New York buyers purchasing above $1,000,000 must also budget for New York's progressive Mansion Tax (starting at 1.0% up to 3.9% in NYC) plus Mortgage Recording Tax (~1.8%–1.925% in NYC), making upfront closing costs among the highest in the nation.

4 Smart Ways New York Homebuyers Cut Monthly Costs in 2026

  • Register for New York's STAR (School Tax Relief) credit immediately after closing if your household income is under $500,000 to receive an annual property tax rebate check.
  • For NYC condo/co-op buyers, enter the full monthly common charge / maintenance fee into the HOA field—co-op maintenance already bundles building property taxes.
  • If refinancing in New York, ask your lender about a CEMA (Consolidation, Extension, and Modification Agreement) loan to avoid paying NY's steep Mortgage Recording Tax a second time!

Frequently Asked Questions: New York Mortgage & Property Taxes

How much is the monthly mortgage on a $500,000 house in New York in 2026?

At 6.5% APR with 20% down, a $500,000 New York home averages roughly $3,274/month (including $2,528 P&I, $583 property tax at 1.40%, and $163 insurance)—or ~$3,834/month with 10% down including PMI.

What is a New York CEMA refinance?

A CEMA lets New York homeowners assign their existing mortgage to a new lender during a refinance so you only pay Mortgage Recording Tax on any NEW money borrowed, saving thousands in closing costs.

Do New York buyers pay the Mansion Tax on a $500,000 home?

No. New York's Mansion Tax begins only on residential purchases of $1,000,000 or more.

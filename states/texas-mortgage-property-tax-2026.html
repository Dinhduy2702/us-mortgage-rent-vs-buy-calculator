How Texas Property Taxes Impact Your 2026 Mortgage

Because Texas charges 0% state personal income tax, Texas school districts, counties, and cities fund public infrastructure primarily through property taxes—averaging roughly 1.63% effective rate statewide in 2026 (and 1.8%–2.4% in newer Austin, Dallas-Fort Worth, and Houston Municipal Utility Districts / MUDs). Fortunately, Texas voters dramatically expanded the mandatory Homestead Exemption to $100,000 off school district taxable value and capped annual homestead appraisal increases at 10% per year.

Real $500,000 Monthly Payment Breakdown in Texas

On a $500,000 home in Texas at 6.5% APR, property taxes (~$679/month) and wind/hail homeowners insurance (~$258/month) add over $935/month ON TOP of your Principal & Interest payment! That is why a $500,000 home in Texas costs roughly $3,466/month all-in with 20% down—about $450/month MORE than a $500k home in California, even though Texas has 0% state income tax. 4 Smart Ways Texas Homebuyers Cut Monthly Costs in 2026

  • File your free Texas Residence Homestead Exemption with your County Appraisal District immediately after closing to claim the $100,000 school-tax reduction and the 10% annual appraisal cap.
  • Protest your county property tax appraisal every spring (May 15 deadline)—Texas homeowners who protest with recent comps routinely cut $500–$1,500/year off escrow payments.
  • Check whether a suburban new-build lies inside a MUD (Municipal Utility District), which can add 0.50%–0.90% on top of standard county property tax rates.
  • Compare homeowners insurance quotes with 1% vs. 2% wind/hail deductibles carefully in Dallas, Houston, and coastal counties.

Frequently Asked Questions: Texas Mortgage & Property Taxes

How much are property taxes on a $500,000 home in Texas in 2026?

At the 1.63% statewide effective average, property taxes on a $500,000 Texas home run roughly $8,150/year (~$679/month), though filing the $100,000 Homestead Exemption lowers the school-district portion significantly.

Does Texas cap how much my property tax assessment can rise each year?

Yes. Once your Texas Homestead Exemption is active, your home's appraised value for tax purposes cannot increase by more than 10% per year (excluding new improvements).

Why is Texas homeowners insurance higher than the US average?

Severe hailstorms in North/Central Texas and hurricane exposure along the Gulf Coast push average Texas homeowners insurance to ~$2,800–$3,500+/year on a $500k home.
